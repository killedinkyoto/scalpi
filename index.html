01 · The Root Cause

What is really
happening beneath
your scalp.

The hair you see is already dead. The only living part is the follicle: the tiny root buried under your skin. During perimenopause, your estrogen drops fast, and your scalp ages faster too. The skin gets thinner. Small pockets of irritation start building up. And the follicle stops getting what it needs to grow.

The Trapped Barrier
The Plastic Wrap Over Your Scalp
Most shampoos strip away your scalp's natural oil using a harsh detergent called SLS. Conditioners try to fix it with silicone, which coats your scalp like plastic wrap. That coating traps oil and irritation inside your pores instead of letting it out.
Premature Aging
Your Follicles Are Aging Too Fast
That trapped oil breaks down into harmful compounds. Those compounds cause ongoing irritation, and over time your follicle cells simply stop dividing. Once that happens, follicles shut down years before they actually should.
The Stop Signal
The Protein Shutting Down Your Growth
When your scalp is under stress, your follicle's control center makes more of a protein called DKK-1. Think of it as a stop sign: it tells your follicle to quit growing. The good news is this can be measured, and it can be reversed.
The Penetration Problem
Why Nothing Has Worked

How is Scalpi different from rosemary oil or other topical hair serums?
+
Regular serums and oils cannot get past your skin's outer layer. Studies show only 0.3% of what is in those products reaches the layer where your follicles actually live. Scalpi uses tiny capsules that blend into your skin, travel down the follicle, and release their ingredients right at the root, reaching about 70% of follicle depth. No regular topical can do that.
Will it make my hair greasy or require changes to my styling routine?
+
No. Unlike castor, jojoba, or coconut oil, which sit on top of your hair and weigh it down, Scalpi fully absorbs into your scalp in under 60 seconds. It does not travel up the hair shaft, so there is no residue, no weight, and no greasy look. You can style your hair like normal the next morning.
I've tried Nutrafol and Viviscal. Why would this be different?
+
Hair supplements have to pass through your liver before reaching your scalp at all. Whatever survives that trip then travels through your bloodstream and competes with every other organ in your body. Only a tiny fraction ever makes it to your scalp, and none of it specifically targets your follicles. Scalpi skips your stomach and liver completely. It delivers ingredients straight to the follicle root, with 2.7 times better absorption than regular creams.
How long before I see results?
+
Hair grows in cycles, so results take a bit of patience no matter what you use. Most people notice less shedding within 2 to 4 weeks. Visible thickness usually shows up between months 3 and 6, once follicles complete a full growth cycle. That is why the 6-bottle option covers the full 6-month course.
Is there a risk of a "dread shed" like I experienced with rosemary oil?
+
No. The dread shed from heavy oils happens because the oily film blocks your pores and feeds scalp yeast, which causes irritation and shedding. Scalpi works differently. It does not sit on your skin or block your follicles. It is built to undo that suffocating effect, not cause it.
Is this safe if I have thyroid concerns or shellfish allergies?
+
Yes. Scalpi has zero kelp, iodine, shark cartilage, or oyster powder, the ingredients that cause thyroid issues and shellfish allergy reactions in supplements like Viviscal. It is applied to your scalp and does not enter your bloodstream, so there is no organ stress to worry about.
