What Your Shampoo And “Natural” Hair Oils Are Actually Doing To Your Scalp

Your hair follicles are live skin organs — structurally identical to the skin on your face. They need to breathe. Oxygen, nutrients, blood flow.

But the average woman over 45 has spent thirty years coating her scalp in silicones, heavy carrier oils, and synthetic conditioners. Sodium Lauryl Sulfate. Dimethicone. Castor. Coconut. Jojoba. Every one of those ingredients leaves a film.

That film is occlusive. Think plastic wrap — sealed across your scalp. The follicles underneath can't breathe. Sebum gets trapped. It oxidizes into inflammatory compounds called lipid peroxides. Those compounds cause chronic micro-inflammation around every follicle.

The inflammation triggers a process called follicular senescence. Your follicles stop producing new hair. They shrink. They miniaturize. Eventually they shut down for good.

That's why your bloodwork looks fine and your hair keeps falling out. That's why rosemary oil made it worse — it added more occlusion, then triggered what menopause forums call the “dread shed,” your follicles literally panicking and dumping weak hair to try to breathe. That's why oral biotin and collagen never reach your scalp: your gut can't deliver nutrients through a sealed door.

Your follicles aren't dead. They're suffocating. Starving. Shrinking. And you cannot fix that with anything that sits on top of the seal.
